Privacy Policy

← Mental Math: Focus Trainer

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Mental Math has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, or any other personal details. Your exercise history stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. Here is exactly what the app uses.

The short version: Your practice results, streaks, and settings live on your device. If iCloud is on, your results and custom exercises are also kept, encrypted, in your private iCloud so your other devices see them — we don't run a server and can't see them. The only thing that leaves your phone is anonymous, aggregate app-usage and crash information. Payments for the optional Pro subscription are handled entirely by Apple.

1. Your Practice Data: Your Device and Your iCloud

Everything the app records about your training — exercise results, scores, streaks, custom exercises, and preferences — is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad (and in a shared app container so widgets can show your streak).

If you are signed in to iCloud, your exercise results and custom exercises are also synced, encrypted, through your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit), so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That database belongs to your Apple Account: we have no server and no access to it, and it is never shared with any other third party. If iCloud is off, nothing leaves your device.

2. Analytics & Diagnostics

To fix bugs and understand which features are used, Mental Math collects a small amount of anonymous, aggregate information through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics):

  • Usage events — e.g. app opened, a session completed, a feature used, and error codes.
  • Crash reports and basic performance/device information (e.g. OS version, device model, approximate region).
  • A Firebase installation identifier and a random per-install device identifier (Apple's "identifier for vendor") used to group these events and crash reports.
This analytics stream never includes your actual answers, scores, or practice history. It is not linked to your name or identity (Mental Math has no accounts), and it is not used to track you across other apps or websites — no advertising identifier (IDFA) is used.

This processing is handled by Google. See Firebase Privacy & Security and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Pro Subscription)

The optional Pro subscription is sold through the Apple App Store and processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details, billing address, or card number. The app checks your subscription status on-device through Apple's StoreKit. You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

4. Practice Reminders (Notifications)

If you enable reminders, they are scheduled as local notifications on your device. There is no push server, and enabling reminders sends nothing to us. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

5. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number.
  • No precise or background location.
  • No advertising identifier and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No upload of your practice results or answers to us — they only ever go to your own iCloud.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

6. Data Retention

Your practice data is kept on your device while the app is installed and, if iCloud sync is on, in your private iCloud until you delete it there. Deleting the app removes the copy on that device; the iCloud copy stays so your history comes back when you reinstall. Anonymous analytics and crash data are retained by Google according to their policies. Purchase records are kept by Apple under your Apple Account.

7. Children's Privacy

Mental Math is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Delete the app to remove practice data from that device. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Storage → Mental Math and delete its data. Because analytics is anonymous and not tied to an identity, there is no personal profile of you for us to hold, access, or delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will appear here with a new "Last updated" date.
